1. Site Name

T.Q. Creator Hub

T.Q. Creator Hub is a website designed to help content creators explore technology, discover creative tools, develop content ideas, and connect with opportunities online. The name combines my Tech Queen (T.Q.) identity with my vision of creating a space where content creators can explore technology, share ideas, learn new skills, and grow.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of T.Q. Creator Hub is to provide information and resources that help content creators use technology to develop, share, and promote their ideas.

The website will explore topics such as digital devices, content-creation tools, social media, personal websites, and online networking. It will also explain how creators can use technology and digital platforms to connect with others and create opportunities by sharing their ideas and work online.

An interactive content idea generator will allow visitors to explore and discover inspiration for their next creative project.

3. Scenarios

The following questions represent things visitors might want to learn when using T.Q. Creator Hub:

  • What digital tools and devices can I use to create quality content?
  • How can I promote my content and connect with other creators online?
  • How can I generate fresh ideas when I don't know what to post?
  • How can I create my own website to showcase my work?

4. Color Schema

T.Q. Creator Hub will use a pink-and-black color scheme to create a modern, feminine, and technology-focused visual identity.

Midnight Black - #171717

Used for the header, navigation, footer, headings, and important text.

Blush Pink - #E8A6BC

Used for buttons, highlights, links, and decorative accents.

Soft Pink - #FCEEF3

Used for the main page background and selected content sections.

5. Typography

Poppins

Poppins will be used for the main headings, section titles, and navigation. It gives the website a clean and modern appearance.

Arial

Arial will be used for paragraphs, descriptions, labels, and general body text to keep the content clear and easy to read.
